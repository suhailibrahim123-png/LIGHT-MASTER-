Questions

Will Light Master LEDs trigger an error message on my dashboard?

No. Our bulbs and Bi-LED modules include CANBUS micro-decoders that mimic OEM electrical resistance, preventing bulb-out warnings, error codes and flicker on European, Japanese, Korean and Indian vehicles.

How does the 2-year replacement warranty work?

If a bulb or projector fails, flickers or suffers diode degradation within 24 months of the invoice date, submit a claim on our warranty page or WhatsApp +91 95444 88144 and we process a replacement unit.

Will upgrading to Bi-LED blind oncoming drivers?

No. Light Master Bi-LED projectors use precision optical cutoff shields so the light stays on the road, markers and kerbs and the opposing lane stays dark.

Do you supply wholesale B2B pricing for garages and dealers?

Yes. We supply wholesale price matrices, shop demo fixtures and white-label OEM supply for registered workshops across India and the GCC. Message us on WhatsApp for the trade list.

Will LED bulbs work in my halogen housing?

Often yes, but the beam only stays safe with correct alignment. Send your model and year on WhatsApp and we will advise.

Do you ship outside India?

We serve Oman, UAE and Qatar. Message us with your location for delivery options.
